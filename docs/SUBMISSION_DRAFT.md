**Perps Execution Inspector — understand what actually happened to your dreamDEX order.** A trader can have gas in one wallet and trading margin in another. PEI keeps those roles distinct, checks supported orders before signing, and separates receipt fills, latest remainder and block-level exposure in one inspectable report. A chain/hash link can be checked without the sender's journal or wallet, subject to site access. From the report, inspect the correct account and model a focused market shock against an exactly reconciled frozen baseline. Category: trader tools, for traders, bot builders and support engineers troubleshooting uncertain executions.

Build evidence: pinned SDK 0.32.1, verified public chain/indexer reads, automated regression evidence in the current improvement handoff, and no new contracts. Synthetic wallet tests are labelled; real PEI-origin signing evidence remains unverified. Before submission attach the actual public GitHub/commit, accessible demo, original PEI attempt plus canonical report, two or three real X URLs, and confirmed cutoff/whitelist. Single direct/delegated pool calls are supported; arbitrary smart-account batches are not. Three-person usability results must remain unperformed until actual people participate.
